What You Can Do

  • Find a bin
  • Take your litter with you
  • Pick up one extra piece
  • Look after nature
  • Recycle when you can

Did You Know?

  • Over 8 million tonnes of plastic enter the ocean each year
  • Most litter starts on land and travels to waterways
  • Plastic harms birds, turtles and sea animals
  • Small changes from many people can create big impact
