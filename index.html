Meet Kimmy

Kimmy is a kind-hearted girl who loves her town and her planet. When she discovers litter on her favourite path, she decides it’s time to take action.

With help from her friends and her magical sparkling bag, Kimmy begins a joyful clean-up mission that inspires the whole community.

Help Save Our Planet

Small actions can make a big difference

What You Can Do

  • Find a bin.
  • Take your litter with you.
  • Pick up one extra piece.
  • Look after nature.
  • Recycle when you can.

Why It Matters

  • Most litter starts on land and travels to the ocean.
  • Plastic can harm birds, turtles and sea animals.
  • When one species is harmed, others can be affected too.
  • Small actions today help protect tomorrow.
