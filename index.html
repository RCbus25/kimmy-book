About Kimmy

Inspired by real-life litter collection walks, Kimmy and the Little Planet Helpers encourages children to care for nature through kindness, teamwork and simple actions.

Perfect for ages 3–8, the books combine beautiful illustrations with positive environmental messages and gentle learning opportunities.
